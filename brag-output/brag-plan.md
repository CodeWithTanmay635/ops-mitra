# Brag Plan: OpsMitra

## Creative Angle & Story
**Tone**: Premium SaaS product-launch style (clean, modern, restrained, professional).
**Theme**: Turn business data into the next decision. "Your next business decision, not another dashboard."
**Visual Language**: Warm neutral / muted orange matching OpsMitra UI. Focus exclusively on product UI. No neon/cyberpunk/robots. 
**Duration**: 45-60 seconds.

## Storyboard

**Scene 1: The Hook (0-5s)**
- **Visual**: Subtle, clean typography on a warm neutral background.
- **Text**: "MSMEs don't need another dashboard." (Pause) "They need to know what to act on next."
- **Audio/Narration**: (Subtle intro sweep/chime) 

**Scene 2: Introduction (5-10s)**
- **Visual**: OpsMitra logo/title elegantly resolves.
- **Text**: "OpsMitra. Your next business decision, not another dashboard."
- **Audio**: Soft professional tone transition.

**Scene 3: The Intelligence (10-25s)**
- **Visual**: Show the actual Dashboard UI. Zoom/focus on Mehta Traders card.
- **Detail Elements**:
  - ₹1,24,000 outstanding
  - 43 days overdue
  - Risk 95 HIGH
  - Priority 72
  - Action: Escalate immediately
- **Text**: "Identifies highest-priority actions automatically."

**Scene 4: The Simulation (25-45s)**
- **Visual**: The "What-If" simulation UI.
- **Text**: "What if this customer pays ₹75,000?"
- **Detail Elements (Before & After)**:
  - Outstanding: ₹1,24,000 → ₹49,000
  - Risk: 95 HIGH → 40 MEDIUM
  - Priority: 72 → 45
  - Action: Escalate immediately → Proactive check-in

**Scene 5: Outro (45-50s)**
- **Visual**: OpsMitra logo centered. Clean fade.
- **Text**: "OpsMitra. Turn business data into the next decision."
